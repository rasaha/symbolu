Methodological review memo (docs-only; no experiment, no judges, no artifact/frozen-asset modified). Audits one

## Three separated questions

**A — Instrument quality: was the current evaluator-facing representation shown unsuitable?** **Yes,
demonstrated.** Deterministically: descriptor **length leakage** (vowel 24–36 vs consonant 37–121 chars,
disjoint → exposes the C/V skeleton); **inventory leakage** (distinct inventories → the unordered bag identifies
each word); **first-position leakage** (first descriptor unique per word); **generic/repetition** (inherent-`a`
→ "restless starting…" recurs in almost every word); **narrative remnants** and **raw Sanskrit terms** in most
consonant descriptors; and **domain mismatch for evaluator interpretation** (affliction descriptors vs ordinary
referents — see the 32-word example table). These are structural/representational facts, not judge results.

**B — Instrument class: can *this* evaluator instrument fairly test H2?** **No — demonstrated for this instrument
as configured.** The failing assumptions: (i) that the frozen **binding-pole** descriptors provide a *principled
semantic basis for choosing an ordinary referent* — they do not; (ii) that the selected words give **order
headroom** — they do not (distinct inventories make the bag decisive); (iii) that a leakage-safe rendering
exists with these descriptors — length/first-position/inventory shortcuts remain. So the *binding-pole →
ordinary-referent* instrument cannot fairly isolate an order effect. This does **not** show that *no* B1.12
instrument could (e.g. a tendency-domain candidate set, both poles, an anagram word set are untested).

**C — Symbol-U theory: were the varṇa mappings shown to be the wrong semantic domain?** **No — not directly
tested.** The mappings are self-consistently a **bipolar tendency (vṛtti)** system (binding + liberating poles).
The experiment used only the binding pole and matched against ordinary referents — an instrument choice. A
failure to match referents is *expected* whether the mapping is meaningful-in-its-own-domain or meaningless, so
the experiment **cannot** adjudicate the theory.

## The core confound (explicit)

Suppose the mapping genuinely encodes latent psychological tendencies rather than dictionary definitions. The
current experiment **cannot distinguish**:
1. *"the mapping is wrong"* from
2. *"the mapping may be internally meaningful but this evaluator representation (binding pole, ordinary-referent
   candidates) is unsuitable."*
Both hypotheses predict the same observation — failure to identify ordinary referents from binding descriptors.
The example review sharpens this: positive-valence referents (peace, joy, happiness, truth, virtue) receive the
*opposite* (negative) descriptors, which is exactly what a **binding-pole** reading would produce regardless of
whether the mapping is valid — so the failure indicts the **pole/candidate choice**, not necessarily the mapping.
The two remain **experimentally confounded.**

## Revised concluding paragraph (replaces the over-reaching one; for future reports)

> This iteration demonstrates, deterministically, that **the current B1.12 G1 evaluator instrument — ordered
> frozen *binding-pole* varṇa descriptors matched against ordinary-referent candidates — is unsuitable**: it
> exhibits length, first-position, and inventory leakage; its descriptors are generic and repetitive
> (inherent-`a` recurs pervasively); and, being affliction/tendency glosses, they provide no principled basis
> for identifying ordinary referents (across 32 attested words, none was naturally identifiable; positive
> referents received actively contradictory negative descriptors). The instrument is therefore
> `G1_BLOCKED_DESCRIPTOR_QUALITY` for this representation. The result is **scoped to instrument suitability**. It
> does **not** show that the underlying Symbol-U varṇa mappings are defective or in the "wrong" domain: those
> mappings are a bipolar tendency system, and this experiment fixed one pole and one (referent) candidate domain,
> so it **cannot distinguish** "the mapping is wrong" from "the mapping may be internally meaningful but this
> representation is unsuitable" — the two remain confounded. It also does **not** test whether ordered varṇa
> composition carries information (H2 was never probed — the instrument was blocked before any usability probe),
> and it does **not** explain B1.10's null (a separate experiment). What is warranted is narrow and instrumental;
> everything beyond it is untested.

## Should any prior conclusion be narrowed? — Yes

- **Narrow:** the V1.2 report's framing that the mappings "are affliction-tendency glosses, not referent
  descriptors — consistent with B1.10's null" over-reaches at the theory level. Retain the **observation**
  (descriptors are affliction-domain and mismatched to the referent candidates); **withdraw** the implication
  that this reflects a defect in Symbol-U or bears on B1.10's mechanism. Statements 5–8 above are not supported
  by this experiment.

## Should any conclusion be strengthened? — One clarification, not a strengthening

- The **instrument-unsuitability** findings (A, B, statements 1–4) are solid and may be stated with confidence.
- The **confound** and the **polarity/candidate-domain** cause were implicit before; making them explicit is a
  legitimate sharpening of the caveat (it constrains, rather than expands, the claim).
