**What varies, and what does not (critical).** A word's **two packets are fixed by its varṇas** and are
**context-invariant** — the binding packet and the liberating packet are the same regardless of which sentence the
word sits in. **Only the context changes, and the context decides which pole *should* fit.** So the prepared judge
question is a *fit-to-condition* rating, not a definition check:

> **"How well does this packet describe the inner experiential weather / source-condition underlying this word in
> this context?"**

Expected pattern if pole logic is legible: binding context rates the **binding** packet higher than the
liberating packet; liberating context rates the **liberating** packet higher. Equal ratings = an honest null.

## 1. happy — sequence `ha, pa, pa, ya`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Binding / other-conditioned context** | *"He was happy only because he had beaten his rival and could watch the man's face fall."* |
| **Liberating / self-grounded context** | *"She was happy sitting alone at dawn, wanting nothing and comparing herself to no one."* |
| **Expected binding packet (v3)** | `[ha]` outward / visible vision — fixation on the manifest, outward-facing world · `[pa]` ghṛṇā — the fetter of hatred / revulsion; downward pull toward the crude · `[ya]` aviśvāsa — self-doubt that cannot commit; distrust of self and others |
| **Expected liberating packet (v3)** | `[ha]` intuitional vision (parā-vidyā) — inner, subtle seeing/knowing · `[pa]` the upward turn — goodwill that dissolves revulsion; anurakti consummating in devotion · `[ya]` self-efficacy — steady trust in oneself, others and the path |
| **Why binding-context → binding packet** | Rival-conditioned joy is outward-fixated (`ha` outward vision), carries envy/ill-will toward the rival (`pa` ghṛṇā/revulsion), and rests on an external scoreboard rather than self-trust (`ya` aviśvāsa). |
| **Why liberating-context → liberating packet** | Alone-at-dawn, wanting-nothing joy is inward seeing (`ha` intuitional vision), an upward self-contented turn (`pa` upward turn), grounded in self-trust needing no comparison (`ya` self-efficacy). |
| **Ambiguity warning** | Low. Strong three-facet alignment on both poles. `pa` (revulsion↔upward-turn) is the sharpest discriminator. |

## 2. love — sequence `la, va`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Binding / other-conditioned context** | *"His love demanded she prove it daily, and curdled into jealousy whenever she looked away."* |
| **Liberating / self-grounded context** | *"Her love asked for nothing back; it simply wished the other well and let him go."* |
| **Expected binding packet (v3)** | `[la]` kruratā — cruelty; crude, cruel thought and behaviour · `[va]` ensconcement gone rigid — over-holding, clinging to one's holding; stuck fixity |
| **Expected liberating packet (v3)** | `[la]` compassion (karuṇā) that protects the vulnerable — the will to reduce another's misery · `[va]` dharma — the sustaining principle; unbroken movement toward subtlety |
| **Why binding-context → binding packet** | Possessive, jealousy-curdled love is over-holding and clinging (`va` rigid ensconcement) and turns harmful/controlling toward its object (`la` kruratā). |
| **Why liberating-context → liberating packet** | Wishing-well-and-letting-go love is protective goodwill (`la` karuṇā) held as a sustaining, non-grasping stance (`va` dharma). |
| **Ambiguity warning** | Moderate. `la` binding = **cruelty** is a coarse fit for jealous love (the source-condition is possessive control, not literal cruelty); the clinging `va` facet carries most of the binding signal. Only 2 facets, so one weak facet matters more. |

## 3. peace — sequence `pa, ka`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Binding / other-conditioned context** | *"He felt peace only once his opponents were silenced and no one could challenge him."* |
| **Liberating / self-grounded context** | *"Peace settled in her on its own, needing no victory and no one's permission."* |
| **Expected binding packet (v3)** | `[pa]` ghṛṇā — the fetter of hatred / revulsion; downward pull toward the crude · `[ka]` āśā as grasping / clinging hope — goaded toward a specific outcome |
| **Expected liberating packet (v3)** | `[pa]` the upward turn — goodwill that dissolves revulsion; anurakti consummating in devotion · `[ka]` aspiring hope held WITHOUT attachment — releasing the grip on the result |

| **Why liberating-context → liberating packet** | Self-arising peace needing no victory is the upward, goodwill turn (`pa`) and hope/orientation without attachment to any outcome (`ka`). |
| **Ambiguity warning** | Low. Clean `ka` grasping↔non-grasping discriminator directly encodes "needing a victory" vs "needing nothing." (Same sequence as frozen pd-09 peace.) |

## 4. longing — sequence `la, nga, nga`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Binding / other-conditioned context** | *"His longing fixed on possessing her, restless and grasping until she was his."* |
| **Liberating / self-grounded context** | *"Her longing turned inward and upward, a pull toward the Great that asked for no object."* |
| **Expected binding packet (v3)** | `[la]` kruratā — cruelty; crude, cruel thought and behaviour · `[nga]` dambha as vanity — the self displayed and performed for others' eyes |
| **Expected liberating packet (v3)** | `[la]` compassion (karuṇā) that protects the vulnerable · `[nga]` conduct that stands without outward performance — action for its own sake, needing no audience |
| **Why binding-context → binding packet** | Possessive, grasping longing is object-fixated and self-serving; the `nga` vanity/outward-display facet fits craving-to-possess, and `la` marks the harmful, controlling edge. |
| **Why liberating-context → liberating packet** | Objectless inward-upward longing is precisely "conduct without outward performance" (`nga`) held as protective, non-grasping orientation (`la` karuṇā). |
| **Ambiguity warning** | **Higher.** longing's binding source-condition is *grasping/possessiveness*, but neither `la` (cruelty) nor `nga` (vanity) names grasping directly — the fit is oblique. The **liberating** side (`nga` objectless, unwitnessed) fits noticeably better than the binding side does. Discriminating power is asymmetric; flag for reconsideration. |

## 5. devotion — sequence `da, va, ta, na`

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Binding / other-conditioned context** | *"His devotion was performed for the congregation's eyes, hungry for their approval."* |
| **Liberating / self-grounded context** | *"Her devotion was quiet and unwitnessed, offered for its own sake and no one's applause."* |
| **Expected binding packet (v3)** | `[da]` peevishness / reactive irritability — perverse, contrary reactivity · `[va]` ensconcement gone rigid — over-holding, clinging · `[ta]` jāḍya — inertia, staticity, dullness, torpor · `[na]` moha — blind attachment / infatuation; the mind fixated on an object, losing rationality |
| **Expected liberating packet (v3)** | `[da]` forbearance that absorbs provocation — an even, unreactive temper · `[va]` dharma — the sustaining principle; movement toward subtlety · `[ta]` the cessation of dullness — awakening from torpor into expansion · `[na]` de-fascination from false attraction — freedom from fixation |
| **Why binding-context → binding packet** | Approval-hungry, performed devotion is attachment fixated on an audience/outcome (`na` moha), clung to rigidly (`va`), while the self stays externally driven (`ta` torpor, `da` reactivity). |
| **Why liberating-context → liberating packet** | Unwitnessed, for-its-own-sake devotion is de-fascinated from external validation (`na` de-fascination), a sustaining stance (`va` dharma) that is awake, not torpid (`ta`), and even-tempered (`da` forbearance). |
| **Ambiguity warning** | **Mixed.** The strongest binding discriminator, `na` moha↔de-fascination, fits the "hungry for approval" vs "for its own sake" contrast very well. But `da` (peevishness) and `ta` (inertia) are **off-axis** for performative devotion — a performed devotion is neither irritable nor inert. Note the `nga` (vanity/display) facet that would most directly name "performed for others' eyes" is **not in devotion's sequence** (no `ṅa`), so the packet under-serves the exact source-condition. |
