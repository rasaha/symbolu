# B1.1 Ca / Va Functional Distinction — Addendum

## 1. Scope and non-claims

**Addendum only.** Sharpens the B1.1 functional distinction between **Ca (Viveka)** and **Va (Dharma)** so Va
is not framed as "aligning with what is true" (which wrongly implies Va first *verifies* truth). Modifies
**no** JSON, runs **no** model, embedding, generation, or scoring. Does **not** modify B1, change the verdict
(`RANDOM_OR_SCRAMBLED_MATCHES`), or unblock Track B (**BLOCKED**). Reasons **within the framework's own
logic** to label operations correctly; makes **no** claim of ontology validation, Sanskrit privilege, or
semantic truth. **Structure, not validated meaning.**

## 2. Ca as falsehood-discerning Viveka

- **Ca = discerning falsehoods.** Viveka is the capacity to **detect false, mixed, misleading, or unreal
  appearance.**
- **Ca removes the false.**
- **Updated Ca (source-attested pole preserved: Viveka / discernment):**
  - functional operation: **separates truth from falsehood by detecting misperception, distortion, and false
    appearance**
  - contrast boundary: **not Va/dharma, not assent, not acceptance-as-true, not conduct-ordering**

## 3. Va as accepting-as-true / Dharma-ordering

- **Va = accepting-*as*-true** (not accepting/aligning *with* what is true). Dharma is the capacity to
  **grant truth-status / order-status and align conduct around it.**
- **Va installs what is accepted as true into conduct / order.**
- **Updated Va (source-attested pole preserved: Dharma / accepted order / truth-status alignment):**
  - functional operation: **accepts-as-true and organizes conduct around what is granted reality/order
    status**
  - contrast boundary: **not Ca/viveka, not falsehood detection, not analysis, not verification**

## 4. Why Va is not truth verification

Framing Va as "aligning *with what is true*" smuggles in a verification step — as if Va first checks truth,
then aligns. It does not. **Va grants truth-status and orders conduct around it; the checking is Ca's job,
not Va's.** Therefore:

- **Avoid:** Va = aligning with what is true · Va = truth verification · Va = discernment · Va = clarity.
- **Preferred:** Va = accepting / aligning *as* true · Va = truth-assent and conduct-ordering · Va =
  installing accepted truth as governing order.

The two are **complementary, not synonymous**: Ca is *subtractive* (removes the false), Va is *installative*
(grants and orders). They now explicitly boundary each other, which also reduces any Ca↔Va near-synonymy risk
at the embedding gate.

## 5. How weak Ca + strong Va can bind

**Va can bind if what is accepted-as-true is not actually true.** Because Va grants order-status without
verifying, it **depends on Ca / Viveka for purification** of its input:

- **weak Ca + strong Va → falsehood becomes accepted as truth**, then installed as governing order — a
  strongly-held, well-organized error.
- This is the same "a positive pole can still bind" logic as the guṇa (`29cb457`) and vidyā (`ac88dc5`)
  addenda: Dharma-as-order is not automatically liberating; a mis-purified Va binds conduct around a
  falsehood.

**Open consideration (NOT decided here):** this binding dependency is a candidate reason to add **Va** to the
human-review set alongside {Ra, Sa, Śa, Ha, Kṣa} before freeze. This addendum **does not** move Va; it
records the question for the freeze review. (Ca remains a discernment function; not flagged.)
