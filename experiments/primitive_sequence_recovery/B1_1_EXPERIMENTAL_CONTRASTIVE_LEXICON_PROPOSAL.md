# B1.1 Experimental Contrastive Lexicon — PROPOSAL (draft, not applied)

## 1. Scope and non-claims

**Proposal only.** This drafts a *proposed* experimental contrastive lexicon for a future B1.1. It modifies
**no** JSON, runs **no** model, generation, or scoring. It does **not** modify B1, change the B1 verdict
(`RANDOM_OR_SCRAMBLED_MATCHES`), or unblock Track B (**BLOCKED**). It makes **no** claim of ontology
validation, Sanskrit privilege, or semantic truth. The rewritten counter-poles below are **experimental
interpretive renderings authored to be contrastive and evaluable — not classical Sanskrit meanings.**
**Structure, not validated meaning.**

## 2. Rewrite rule

- **Preserve the source-attested pole** (the classical acoustic-root propensity, per the committed decision
  memo and its Appendix A) **wherever it sits.**
- **Rewrite only the derived interpretive counter-pole.**
- **All rewritten counter-poles are experimental renderings** — labeled as such, carrying no source
  attestation.
- The goal is **contrastivity and evaluability** (so a random draw R lands on a *different, distinguishable*
  operation than the correct one), **not classical authority.** A B1.1 pass would be
  `LIMITED_GENERATION_UTILITY` for the revised pipeline only.

## 3. Protected source-attested poles

**27 ordinary consonants — protected pole = binding/worldly vrtti** (rewrite the liberated counter-pole):

Pha · Ba · Bha · Ma · Ya · La · Ṣa.

principle; *both* pole-assignments interpretive).

## 4. Four-field format — proposed entries (34 consonants)

> `source-attested pole` = classical, preserved. `experimental counter-pole` = rewritten interpretive
> rendering. `functional operation` = the distinct verb the varṇa performs. `contrast boundary` = the
> specific siblings it must be told apart from.

### 4a. Ordinary consonants (protected = binding pole; counter-pole rewritten)

**Ka**
- source-attested pole: Āśā — hope / forward-grasping for an outcome (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: release of attachment to a specific outcome
- functional operation: lets go of the *desired result* while continuing the effort toward it
- contrast boundary: not object-renunciation (Gha), not craving-cessation (Dha), not disenchantment (Na)

**Kha**
- source-attested pole: Cintā — impersonal worry over open contingencies (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: composure inside unresolved, impersonal uncertainty
- functional operation: holds an unsettled, impersonal situation open without forcing premature resolution
- contrast boundary: not self-efficacy (Ya), not threat-steadiness (Pha), not firmness-under-strain (Cha)

**Ga**
- source-attested pole: Ceṣṭā — effort / striving to arouse dormant potential (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: poised rest that halts striving without going inert
- functional operation: ceases exertion into a stable, alert repose
- contrast boundary: not dull inertia (Ta), not outcome-release (Ka), not containment (Ma)

**Gha**
- source-attested pole: Mamatā — possessive love-bond, mine-ness (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: caring without possessing
- functional operation: keeps the loved object on an open hand — loves it while relinquishing ownership
- contrast boundary: not outcome-release (Ka), not thirst-cessation (Dha), not indifference/neglect (Ba)

**Ṅa**
- source-attested pole: Dambha — vanity, outward display/pretense (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: dropping the performance of oneself for others' eyes
- functional operation: removes the *outward self-display*, letting conduct stand unperformed
- contrast boundary: not deflation of the inner I (Ja), not inner/outer honesty (Ña)

**Cha**
- source-attested pole: Vikalatā — nervous breakdown, collapse of function (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: steadiness that keeps function intact under strain
- functional operation: holds the mind operational under a load that would otherwise break it
- contrast boundary: not threat-steadiness (Pha), not forbearance (Da), not poised rest (Ga)

**Ja**
- source-attested pole: Ahaṁkāra — ego inflation, inflated I-claim (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: deflating the inflated I-claim over events
- functional operation: withdraws the "I did / I control this" from one's account of events
- contrast boundary: not outward display-dropping (Ṅa), not honesty (Ña)

**Jha**
- source-attested pole: Lobha / Lolupatā — greed, avarice, hoarding acquisition (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: sufficiency — releasing the hoarding grip at enough
- functional operation: stops accumulation at sufficiency and releases the surplus grip
- contrast boundary: not thirst-cessation (Dha), not non-possessive love (Gha), not outcome-release (Ka)

**Ña**
- source-attested pole: Kapaṭatā — hypocrisy, concealed duplicity (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: transparent alignment of inner intent and outer act
- functional operation: makes inner intent and outer act coincide, removing concealment
- contrast boundary: not display-dropping (Ṅa), not I-claim deflation (Ja)

**Ṭa**
- source-attested pole: Vitarka — overstatement, garrulous exaggeration (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: measured speech saying only what is warranted
- functional operation: trims expression to exactly the warranted amount
- contrast boundary: not honesty of intent (Ña), not indulgence-restraint (Ma), not poised rest (Ga)

**Ṭha**
- source-attested pole: Anutāpa — repentance, remorse over one's own past act (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: self-directed release of remorse into self-acceptance
- functional operation: discharges remorse over *one's own* past act into acceptance
- contrast boundary: not other-directed forbearance (Da), not shame-release (Ḍa)

**Ḍa**
- source-attested pole: Lajjā — shyness / social self-consciousness (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: acting unhindered by social shame
- functional operation: releases the inhibiting grip of others' regard on one's action
- contrast boundary: not threat-steadiness (Pha), not remorse-release (Ṭha)

**Ḍha**
- source-attested pole: Piśunatā — sadistic cruelty, malice, slander (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: compassion that answers malice with protection
- functional operation: turns malice-energy toward *shielding the maligned* (social harm)
- contrast boundary: not protection of the physically weak (La), not goodwill against revulsion (Pa)

**Ṇa**
- source-attested pole: Īrṣyā — envy (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: gladness at another's good fortune
- functional operation: converts the sting at another's fortune into shared gladness
- contrast boundary: not goodwill against revulsion (Pa), not compassion for suffering (Ḍha/La)

**Ta**
- source-attested pole: Jāḍya / Nidrā — inertia, dullness, deep sleep (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: rousing from torpor into active alertness
- functional operation: lifts *dullness/sleep* into active alertness
- contrast boundary: not breaking a hypnotic spell (Bha), not poised rest (Ga), not discrimination (Ca)

**Tha**
- source-attested pole: Viṣāda — melancholy, dejection (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: warm uplift that lifts dejection
- functional operation: raises one's *own* dejection into affectionate buoyancy
- contrast boundary: not gladness at others' good (Ṇa), not goodwill toward others (Pa)

**Da**
- source-attested pole: Krodha / Karkaśatā — peevishness, irritability (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: other-directed forbearance absorbing provocation
- functional operation: absorbs *present provocation from another* without discharging it back
- contrast boundary: not self-remorse-release (Ṭha), not shame-release (Ḍa), not poised rest (Ga)

**Dha**
- source-attested pole: Tṛṣṇā — craving, thirst for acquisition (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: extinguishing the acquisitive thirst at its root
- functional operation: quenches the *thirst-to-acquire* itself (not a single object)
- contrast boundary: not outcome-release (Ka), not object non-possession (Gha), not anti-hoarding (Jha)

**Na**  *(Viveka collision — rewritten side; Ca's Viveka is protected)*
- source-attested pole: Moha — blind attachment, infatuation (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: disenchantment — seeing through a binding fixation
- functional operation: dispels the *enchantment* that binds one to a fixation
- contrast boundary: **not Ca's analytic discrimination (Viveka, protected)**, not craving-cessation (Dha),
  not outcome-release (Ka)

**Pa**
- source-attested pole: Ghṛṇā — hatred, revulsion (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: goodwill that dissolves revulsion
- functional operation: converts *revulsion toward another* into active goodwill
- contrast boundary: not compassion for the suffering (Ḍha/La), not gladness at fortune (Ṇa)

**Pha**
- source-attested pole: Bhaya — fear of threat/danger (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: steadiness meeting a genuine threat
- functional operation: meets a *real danger* without collapse or flight
- contrast boundary: not social-shame release (Ḍa), not composure under uncertainty (Kha), not
  firmness-under-strain (Cha)

**Ba**
- source-attested pole: Avajñā — indifference, neglect of the valuable (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: regard that recognizes and attends to worth
- functional operation: attends to and honors what has real value instead of neglecting it
- contrast boundary: not goodwill against revulsion (Pa), not gladness at fortune (Ṇa)

**Bha**
- source-attested pole: Mūrcchā — deluded obsession, hypnotic spell (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: breaking the entrancing spell into clear sense
- functional operation: snaps a *hypnotic fixation* and restores clear common sense
- contrast boundary: not lifting dullness/sleep (Ta), not disenchantment from infatuation (Na), not
  discrimination (Ca)

**Ma**
- source-attested pole: Praśraya / Praṇāśa — indulgence / dissolution, giving latitude (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: disciplined containment that holds form
- functional operation: contains and holds a form against over-indulgence and dissolution
- contrast boundary: not measured speech (Ṭa), not forbearance (Da), not poised rest (Ga)

**Ya**  *(Viśvāsa collision — Ya side)*
- source-attested pole: Aviśvāsa — lack of confidence in self and others (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: self-efficacy — the confidence to commit and to rely
- functional operation: musters the *self-trust* needed to commit to an action
- contrast boundary: not composure under impersonal uncertainty (Kha), not threat-steadiness (Pha)

**La**  *(Karuṇā collision — La side)*
- source-attested pole: Krūratā — cruelty, physical harm to the weak (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: compassion that protects the physically vulnerable
- functional operation: turns harming-energy toward *protecting the physically weak*
- contrast boundary: not shielding the maligned from slander (Ḍha), not goodwill against revulsion (Pa)

**Ṣa**
- source-attested pole: Kāma / Tamoguṇa — physical desire, static inertia (binding)
- experimental counter-pole: transmutation of raw desire into grounded drive
- functional operation: channels *raw sensory desire* into a grounded, contained drive
- contrast boundary: not thirst-cessation (Dha), not anti-hoarding sufficiency (Jha), not non-possession (Gha)

### 4b. Exception consonants (protected = positive/neutral pole; the *negative* counter-pole rewritten)

**Ca**  *(Viveka collision — protected side)*
- source-attested pole: **Viveka — discrimination of real/unreal, right/wrong (PROTECTED, classical)**
- experimental counter-pole: Aviveka rendered as *undifferentiated conflation* (collapsing distinct categories)
- functional operation (of the protected pole): separates real from unreal, right from wrong — analytic discrimination
- contrast boundary: Ca's discrimination is **not Na's disenchantment** (Na releases a fixation; Ca
  *separates categories*); not lifting dullness (Ta); not breaking a spell (Bha)

**Va**
- source-attested pole: **Dharma — ensconcement in one's original/sustaining stance (PROTECTED)**
- experimental counter-pole: Adharma rendered as *drift from one's sustaining stance*
- functional operation: holds and sustains one's essential order/stance
- contrast boundary: not containment of form (Ma), not poised rest (Ga)

**Sa**
- source-attested pole: **Mokṣa / Sattvaguṇa — unconditioned liberation / clarity (PROTECTED)**
- experimental counter-pole: escapism rendered as *premature withdrawal mimicking release without integration*
- functional operation: resolves into *unconditioned* release (whole, not partial)
- contrast boundary: not outcome-release (Ka), not craving-cessation (Dha), not disenchantment (Na) — those
  are *partial* releases; Sa is unconditioned

**Ha**
- source-attested pole: **Parā-vidyā — intuitional / direct knowledge (PROTECTED)**
- experimental counter-pole: Avidyā rendered as *occluding unknowing*
- functional operation: knows by *direct intuition* rather than mediated inference
- contrast boundary: not mundane/inferential knowledge (Kṣa), not discrimination (Ca)

**Kṣa**
- source-attested pole: **Aparā-vidyā — mundane / material knowledge (PROTECTED)**
- experimental counter-pole: Mithyā-jñāna rendered as *false certainty held against evidence*
- functional operation: knows the measurable/material world by *inference*
- contrast boundary: not intuitional knowledge (Ha), not discrimination (Ca)

**Ra**  *(both poles classically attested — no interpretive rewrite; documented as-is)*
- source-attested pole: **Prāṇaśakti / Agnitattva — vitality / creative fire (PROTECTED)**
- experimental counter-pole: **none synthesized** — the negative pole *Sarvanāśa* (defeatist
  annihilation-thought) is **also classically attested**; kept as-is, not rewritten
- functional operation: kindles / energizes — ignites vital force
- contrast boundary: not directed material drive (Śa), not sustaining stance (Va)

**Śa**  *(neutral principle; both pole-assignments interpretive)*
- source-attested pole: **rajoguṇa (mutative principle) + artha (psychic longing) — NEUTRAL PRINCIPLE (PROTECTED as principle)**
- experimental counter-pole: both framings interpretive — *directed accomplishment* (up) vs *restless
  acquisition* (down); rendered as a single **mutative-drive** axis with two labeled ends
- functional operation: drives mutative, goal-directed pursuit
- contrast boundary: not raw-desire transmutation (Ṣa), not vitality/fire (Ra), not craving-thirst (Dha)

## 6. Counter-pole contrastivity (no generic-virtue collapse)

Each rewritten counter-pole is a **bounded operation**, not a bare virtue word. The previously-overused
generic terms are now differentiated:

- "trust/confidence" → split into Kha *composure-under-uncertainty* vs Ya *self-efficacy*.
- "compassion" → split into Ḍha *shield-the-maligned* vs La *protect-the-physically-weak* (and Pa
  *goodwill-vs-revulsion*, Ṇa *gladness-at-fortune* are distinct warm affects, not "compassion").
- "clarity/discrimination" → Ca *category-separation* (protected) vs Na *disenchantment* vs Ha
  *intuitional knowing* vs Kṣa *inferential knowing*.
- "awareness/awakening" → Ta *lift-dullness* vs Bha *snap-the-spell*.
- "humility" → Ṅa *drop-display* vs Ja *deflate-I-claim*.
- "detachment/release" → the four-way Ka/Gha/Dha/Na split above.
- "courage/fearlessness" → Ḍa *shame-release* vs Pha *threat-steadiness* (and Cha *firmness-under-strain*).

**No bare generic virtue is reused as a counter-pole without a distinct operation + contrast boundary.**

## 7. Bridge-pool generation rule (future B1.1)

The B1.1 bridge pool must be **generated from the four-field entries**, not hand-edited, under:

1. **One-to-one source:** each bridge phrase derives from exactly one (varṇa, pole), carrying its
   *functional operation* — never a bare label.
2. **No collapsed duplicates:** two entries may not yield the same or a near-duplicate bridge phrase (kills
   the B1 68→64 collapse; target 68 distinct phrases if both poles are surfaced).
3. **Operation + boundary preserved:** every bridge phrase encodes the operation and at least the primary
   contrast boundary, so a random draw is a *distinguishably wrong operation*, not a generic mood.
4. **Frozen before B1.1:** the generated pool + its distance report are hash-bound inside the B1.1 freeze
   set (closing the B1 gap where the pool sat outside the frozen artifacts).

## 8. Embedding / non-synonym gate (future check, spec only)

Before B1.1 generation, run a **pre-registered** check over the experimental counter-poles:

- compute **pairwise similarity** among all counter-pole operation strings;
- **flag exact duplicates** (hard fail);
- **flag near-duplicates** above threshold **τ** (human review required);
- **τ and the embedding model are frozen before running** (no post-hoc tuning);
- every flagged pair requires **human adjudication** (rewrite or documented exception).

*Not run here as an embedding check* (no frozen model/threshold yet). A purely local, read-only **token-
overlap** pre-screen over the 34 operation strings may be run to catch obvious collisions; it writes
nothing and is advisory only (results reported alongside this proposal).

## 9. Interaction with the R controls

A contrastive lexicon **feeds** the B1.1 arm set but does **not** replace the stricter controls:

- **R_same_pool** — random draw from *this revised* pool. If the pool is truly contrastive, a random draw is
  now a *distinguishably wrong operation*, so A *can* separate from R_same — **the direct test of whether
  contrastivity was the B1 problem.**
- **R_deranged** — each word gets *another word's* real A-mapping. Holds mapping quality maximal, breaks
  word-fit. The contrastive pool makes this a *fair, hard* control (real operations, wrong word).
- **R_domain_mismatch** — fluent operations sampled from domains distant from the word; the operation +
  boundary structure makes "distant" well-defined.
- **S scrambled** — scrambles the intra-word varṇa order; the pool is unchanged.
- **D dictionary** — plain dictionary meaning; independent of the pool.
- **X neutral** — no conditioning.

**Clarify:** a revised contrastive lexicon **alone is not sufficient.** Even a perfectly contrastive pool
with an arbitrary word→mapping link still yields A ≈ R (that is what **R_deranged** tests). Contrastivity
removes one confound (pool overlap); the stricter R controls remain mandatory.

## 10. Risks and open questions

1. **Over-engineering to beat R.** Authoring ever-finer operations *specifically* to separate from R risks
   fitting the control rather than testing a real channel. Mitigation: freeze the pool + gate **before**
   generation; pre-commit the KILL condition.
2. **Loss of intuitive readability.** Highly differentiated operations may read as stilted, hurting the
   generic evocative quality that A actually showed in B1. Trade-off to watch.
3. **R may remain strong anyway.** Two priors (Track G, B1) say random explains A; contrastivity may not
   change that. B1.1 must be able to **kill**, not only rescue.
4. **Human approval of each counter-pole.** All 27 (+ exception negatives) rewritten renderings need human
   sign-off; they are interpretive, not derived truth.
5. **Blocked-pole conditioning ablation (undecided).** Since binding poles are already distinct,
   conditioning on the binding pole instead of the rewritten liberated pole might weaken R **without** a
   rewrite. Open: include as an exploratory B1.1 arm/ablation, or not?
6. **Ra and Śa are not clean rewrites.** Ra has both poles classically attested; Śa is a neutral principle
   with two interpretive ends. Their treatment (documented above) needs explicit human confirmation.
