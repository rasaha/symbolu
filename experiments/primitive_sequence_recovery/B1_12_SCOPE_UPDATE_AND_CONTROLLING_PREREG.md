Each stage was a legitimate step toward clarifying the research question.

## 3. Final research decision

After this methodology exploration, the research objective for B1.12 has been **frozen** as **Bare-Word Symbolic
Resonance (BSR)**. The governing research question is:

> **Does the stable, ordinary, unqualified meaning of an attested Sanskrit bare word naturally account for the
> frozen pronunciation-derived varṇa mappings without semantic supplementation?**

This is now the **official B1.12 objective**.
