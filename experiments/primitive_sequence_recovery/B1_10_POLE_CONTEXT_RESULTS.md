## 4. Love ambiguity warning (repeated)

**Pre-registered soft flag on `love`:** its binding-pole facet `[la] = kruratā` (cruelty) is a **coarse** fit for
jealous/possessive love — the source-condition is possessive control, not literal cruelty — so the clinging
`[va]` facet was expected to carry most of the binding signal. In this pilot both judges nonetheless rated love's
binding context onto the binding packet fairly high (`Pb|Cb` = 4.5) and love's margin was the **largest** (+9.5);
the pre-flagged weakness did **not** materialize as a weak margin here. The warning **remains on record** — it was
a genuine coarse-fit risk, not retro-justified by the outcome — and love stays labeled a **pre-flagged soft item**.

## 6. Interpretation (bounded; no verdict label)

- This is a **positive descriptive pilot for source-condition / resonance legibility to judges only**: under this
  packet-rating design, two independent real judges reliably rated each word's correct-pole packet as a better
  source-condition fit for its matching context than the cross-pole packet, in both directions, for all three words.
- It **does not prove** ontology, semantic truth, Sanskrit privilege, generation utility, or **word-specific varṇa
  mapping** (the packet is a bag of constituent-varṇa pole-readings; this design cannot attribute the signal to any
  individual varṇa).
- **n = 3 words / 2 judges is NOT a powered inferential result** — it is a descriptive pilot. Positive direction and
  cross-judge consistency are a cue to scale, not a conclusion.
- **Proper next step:** a **preregistered scale-up** with many more same-word two-context items (and more judges),
  with the decision rule fixed in advance — not a stronger claim from these three words.
