# B1 — Native Sanskrit Word-Mapping Review (development-only, docs/data)

**Grade: `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY`.** A mapping-inspection / mechanism-development step: it parses Devanāgarī words with the
frozen Stage-1 parser and shows the **existing** binding/liberating vṛtti attached to each varṇa from the merged
native lexicon (`frozen/varna_native_stage1_merged_v1.json`). It does **not** judge whether any word interpretation
is semantically correct, authors **no** new meaning, and calls **no** word validated. **Structure, not validated
meaning.** No `GENUTILITY_*`; no `ONTOLOGICAL_SIGNAL`. The vowel poles are `AUTHORED_PROVISIONAL` (prior `NO_SIGNAL`).

Generated by `b1_native_word_mapping_review.py`. Full data: `b1_native_word_mapping_review/word_mappings.json` +
`summary.json`.

## Corpus & summary

**153 words** (8 parser fixtures + 35 curated development words + 110 seed list), all round-tripped through the
validated IAST→Devanāgarī helper, exercising short/long vowels, diphthongs, anusvāra, visarga, aspirates,
dental/retroflex, sibilants, conjuncts, gemination, repeated varṇas, and vocalic ṛ (as an explicit missing case).

| word status | count |

| `FULLY_RESOLVED_DEVELOPMENT_GRADE` | 143 |
| `CONTAINS_MISSING_UNIT` | 10 |

- **Mean structural coverage: 0.986** (includes authored vowels — **not** a semantic-validation claim).
- **Mean confirmatory-eligible coverage: 0.54** (consonant backbone only).
- **Every `CONTAINS_MISSING_UNIT` word carries vocalic `ṛ`** (gṛha, hṛdaya, kṛṣṇa, mṛga, mṛtyu, saṃskṛta, tṛṇa,
  vṛkṣa, ṛṣi, …) — the one common phoneme with no merged mapping. `ṝ l̥ l̥̄`/candrabindu did not occur in the corpus.
- **No `CONTAINS_CONTRADICTORY_UNIT`** — the operator ruling kept v3.1's corrected `sha`/`ssa`/`ha`, so no
  contradictory consonant enters any word.

## What this shows (and does not)

- **Every well-formed classical word without vocalic ṛ resolves end-to-end** at development grade: consonants from
  the v3.1 attested backbone (`CONFIRMATORY_BACKBONE`), vowels + anusvāra + visarga from the varṇa-lens
  (`DEVELOPMENT_ONLY`, `AUTHORED_PROVISIONAL`).
- **The two coverage numbers are reported separately on purpose.** 0.986 structural (with authored vowels) vs 0.54
  confirmatory-eligible (consonant backbone). The high structural figure is **not** evidence of meaning.
- **No word is "fully validated."** Any confirmatory claim would first require raising the vowel provenance above
  `AUTHORED_PROVISIONAL` and re-running a pre-registered signal test (given the prior `NO_SIGNAL`), and would need
  the vocalic-ṛ gap closed.

## Single recommended next action

Use this review to drive **mechanism development** — e.g. review whether the ordered binding/liberating vṛtti
sequences read coherently for known Sanskrit words, and decide how a word-level composition rule should consume the
ordered sequence — **without** authoring new vowel meanings and **without** treating any development-grade reading
as validated. Closing the vocalic-ṛ (and ṝ/l̥/l̥̄/candrabindu) gap and raising vowel provenance remain prerequisites
for any confirmatory step.
