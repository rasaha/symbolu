# B1.10 — Blind Context-Authoring Rules (docs-only)

**Purpose:** record the standing rules for authoring the six B1.10 control-extension context sentences (pride,
freedom, patience, courage, control, doubt) **before any blind re-authoring**. These rules exist to close the
context→specific-facet **echo confound** identified in the pre-freeze audit, in which a context sentence written
with knowledge of a word's Tier-3 packet can mirror the carrying facet and inflate `specific_margin` (and therefore
both `increment_over_valence` and `increment_over_source_condition`).

## 1. Blindness requirement

The official contexts MUST be authored by a **packet-naive author or session**. The author MUST NOT see:
- the Tier-3 packets (word-specific v3 renderings),
- the varṇa sequences,
- the existing (current) context sentences,
- the packet paraphrases or the per-varṇa plain-English render map,
- any audit comments,
- any prior strong/weak facet notes or off-axis-facet flags.

Rationale: only an author blind to the facets can produce a context whose fit to the specific packet is *earned by
the word's meaning*, not by mirroring the facet wording. A party that has seen the packets (including this session)
is disqualified from authoring the official sentences.

## 2. Allowed inputs

The blind author may be given ONLY:
- the **target word**,
- a **general binding / other-conditioned (source-conditioned) definition** (word-agnostic: a state contingent on
  others, outcomes, comparison, possession, or approval),
- a **general liberating / self-grounded definition** (word-agnostic: a state resting in itself, needing no outside
  result or approval),
- **sentence-length and naturalness constraints only** (one natural English sentence per pole; no other steering).

No facet text, no varṇa content, no expected-answer hints, no example sentences derived from the packets.

## 3. Single-dominant-condition rule

- Each sentence MUST express **one stable source-condition** (a single stable moment, not a transformation).
- No sentence may **begin in one pole and resolve into the other**.
- Avoid contrastive transition structures that would introduce the opposite condition:
  **but, however, although, even though, yet, despite.**
- These words are **not automatically banned** — they are permitted only when they do **not** introduce a pole
  reversal (e.g. a concessive that reinforces the same pole is acceptable).

## 4. Self-check (record for every sentence)

For each authored sentence, record:
- **intended class:** binding / liberating
- **confidence:** high / medium / low
- **mixed-source-condition detected:** yes / no
- **naturalness:** natural / slightly forced / forced

Governing question: *"Would an independent reader classify this as predominantly binding or predominantly
liberating without ambiguity?"* If not, the sentence fails.

## 5. Failure handling

- If a sentence is **mixed or ambiguous**, **discard it and generate a new sentence.**
- **Never truncate** a sentence to remove the offending clause.
- **Never patch or edit** a sentence after comparing it with the Tier-3 packets.
- **Never tailor** wording to rescue a weak packet.

(Discard-and-regenerate keeps authoring honest: editing toward a pole after seeing the packet is exactly the
tailoring these rules forbid.)

## 6. Independence from the echo confound

- The **current 12 contexts PASS the single-dominant-condition screen** (each is a stable single-pole moment; no
  banned connective introduces a pole reversal — the lone lexical hits, patience-binding "still" = *motionless* and
  courage-liberating "whether or not anyone watched" = a concessive that *reinforces* the self-grounded reading,
  are not reversals).
- **This screen does NOT resolve the separate context→specific-facet echo confound.** Passing "one stable
  condition" says nothing about whether the sentence mirrors the word's Tier-3 facet.
- **freedom and doubt remain the clearest echo concerns:** freedom's binding sentence echoes the `ma` facet
  ("indulged … until nothing held together" ≈ "spills outward until things fall apart"); doubt's sentences echo the
  `ba` and `ta` facets ("dismissing worth" / "dull paralysis" / "weighed things fairly" / "woke her mind up").
- **Official replacement contexts must still be blindly re-authored** per §1–§5. The single-dominant-condition rule
  is an *additional* constraint on that blind authoring, not a substitute for it.
