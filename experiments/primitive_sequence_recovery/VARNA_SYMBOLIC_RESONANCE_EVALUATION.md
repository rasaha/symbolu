# Varṇa Symbolic-Resonance Evaluation (fixed mappings, 60 words)

**`EXPLORATORY / DEVELOPMENT_ONLY / NOT_CONFIRMATORY_EVIDENCE`.** Evaluates **symbolic resonance under the fixed
mappings** — for each word, *does the ordinary bare word naturally account for its mapped affliction, without
adding any adjective, subtype, story, or external actor?* No uniqueness, exclusivity, shuffled-superiority, or
objective-causation requirement. The **relationship type** (embodiment, implication, consequence, opposition,
resolution) is **recorded, not converted into failure**. Barnum risk is a noted limitation, not a verdict rule.

Lexicon `…merged_v3.json` (`65116f37…`); parser `sanskrit_stage1_parser.py`. This supersedes the falsification /
shuffle-verdict framing of the two prior runs (which are retained as records); the shuffle result is demoted to a
limitation note (§ end).

## Scoring (per component, BWR scale)

100 directly/characteristically present · 75 strongly implied · 50 plausible-needs-interpretation · 25
needs-substantial-qualification or an external actor · 0 cannot be supported without added meaning. **Relationship
type** tags *how* the word accounts for the mapping: **EMB** embodiment · **CON** consequence · **IMP**
implication · **OPP** opposition · **RES** resolution · **NR** no natural relationship (needs a story → low). A
mapping **resonates** when the bare word accounts for it (≥75 strongly, 50 partially), by *any* type.

## Result

**Resonance levels:** STRONG (≥75) **18** · MODERATE (50–74) **25** · WEAK (30–49) **13** · MINIMAL (<30) **4**.
**43/60 (72%) resonate at MODERATE-or-STRONG.**

**Relationship types across 160 components:** OPP 35 · EMB 27 · CON 22 · RES 17 · IMP 13 · **NR 46**. The mappings
account for words through a *spread* of relationship types — opposition and embodiment lead, and 46/160 (29%) have
no natural relationship (the honest non-fits, concentrated in neutral objects/body parts).

## Reading the result

- **Strong resonance spans both poles, through different relationship types.** The 18 STRONG words are ~half
  afflictions accounting for their mappings by **embodiment/consequence** (lobha, bhaya, kāma, krodha, īrṣyā,
  moha, mada, dveṣa) and ~half virtues/calm-concepts accounting for them by **opposition/resolution** (mokṣa,
  dhairya, śama, dāna, dayā, karuṇā, ānanda, śānti, tyāga; go by opposition). Under the resonance rule these are
  *equally* successes — the word accounts for its mapping either way.
- **The relationship-type record is where the richness is.** OPP (35) and EMB (27) dominate; RES (17), CON (22),
  IMP (13) fill in. Peace *resolves* grasping; greed *produces* cruelty; gold *implies* clinging; the eye *leads
  to* visual fixation — all legitimate accounts, none downgraded for being "only" opposition or "only"
  consequence.
- **The honest non-fits are the neutral objects and body parts.** MINIMAL/WEAK is dominated by **NR** (no natural
  relationship): jala, ghaṭa, aśva, karṇa, pustaka, candra, and most body parts. Here the bare word does *not*
  account for the mapping without a story ("a jealous person with a stone-like heart"), so they score low — which
  is the principled outcome the resonance rule produces (it does **not** award a vacuous 100 for "doesn't embody
  it").

## Where resonance is genuinely weak (reported, not explained away)

- **Neutral instruments / objects** (body parts, pot, stone, horse, ear, book): the psychic afflictions have no
  natural, story-free relationship to the referent → WEAK/MINIMAL. This is the real boundary of the mapping's
  reach.
- **A few meaning-laden words resonate weakly with their *own* varṇas:** ahiṃsā (37.5), bhakti (41.7),
  buddhi (33.3) — the specific consonants they happen to contain map to afflictions only loosely related to the
  concept. Recorded as-is.

## Barnum risk — noted as a limitation, not a verdict

Because the afflictions are broad and overlapping and five relationship types count as "accounting," a
meaning-laden word can usually find *some* natural relationship to *some* affliction. So high resonance is **not**,
by itself, proof the specific assignment is uniquely right — a different mapping could also resonate (the prior
shuffle probe showed 3/10 afflictions tie). This is a **limitation on interpretation**, disclosed here; per the
task it is **not** used to fail any mapping, and uniqueness/shuffled-superiority is **not** required. Also
single-rater and interpretive — resonance strength is a judgment, not a measurement.

## Bottom line

Under fixed mappings and the resonance rule, **72% of the adversarial sample resonates (MODERATE+STRONG)**, the
mappings account for words through a genuine spread of five relationship types, and the low-resonance cases are
honestly the neutral objects/instruments where no story-free relationship exists. The evaluation is a symbolic-
resonance description, not a falsification test; Barnum and single-rater subjectivity are the disclosed limits.
