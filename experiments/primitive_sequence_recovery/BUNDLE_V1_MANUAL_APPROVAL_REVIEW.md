## 3. Summary

### Classification (pole-DiD)
- **Likely approve: 24 / 24** pole labels are defensible for the word+context (12 liberating, 12 binding). **No pole flip warranted.**
- **Reconsider: 1** — **pd-21 dread** (packet-coherence, not pole flip): the retroflex `da→dda` change swaps in a
  `lajjā` (shame/inhibition) facet that fits a fear word poorly. This is the only item whose sequence moved under the bundle.
- **Reject: 0.**
- **Polysemy watch (context-dependent, not flips): pd-01 surrender, pd-02 release** — a worldly defeat/loss sense
  co-exists; the flipped-pole `sarvanāśa` facet partly resonates with that sense, so the disambiguating context must carry weight.

### Word groups (pole-sanity)
- **Approve as-is: 0 / 24.** None meets 4 clean synonyms + 4 clean opposites.
- **Edit: 10** — pd-01, 02, 05, 06, 09, 11, 12, 15, 17, 18 (have partial material; fixable by curating overrides).
  **pd-06 clarity is the closest to approvable** (4 syn + 3 opp; only drop 'opacity').
- **Reject / rebuild: 14** — pd-03, 04, 07, 08, 10, 13, 14, 16, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 (**no opposites → INT cannot
  form**). Note pd-22 has a full clean synonym set but zero opposites, so it still fails.
- This mirrors the on-disk `needs_manual_replacement` flags (24/24 flagged) — the bundle did not improve
  curation; it only regenerated packet text.

### Items where v3 verbosity may hurt direct judge readability
Facets ≥ 5 with dense Sanskrit + parentheticals: **pd-11 equanimity (6)**, **pd-03 forgiveness, pd-06 clarity,
pd-10 compassion, pd-12 liberation, pd-22 resentment (5 each)**. Long multi-clause facets raise reading load and
dilute the pole signal a judge must weigh.

### Items where the correct-pole packet does not directly describe the target word-family
**All 24 — by construction.** The packet is the pole-reading of each constituent varṇa, not a gloss of the word;
a direct stem/word-family echo appears in **0** items (the lone near-echo, pd-12's "liberates from jāḍya," is
coincidental to the `ta` facet, not word-derived). This is the central legibility caveat: a judge rating
"does this packet fit the word" is rating *varṇa-pole resonance*, **not** a definition of the word. It is the
honest ceiling on what these packets can show — **structure, not validated meaning.**
