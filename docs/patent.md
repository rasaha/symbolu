# FIELD OF DISCLOSURE

1.  The present disclosure generally relates to large language models (LLMs), and more particularly, to a method and system for symbolic reasoning with resonance in the LLMs, which further enables extensions toward Generative Intelligence applications.

# BACKGROUND

1.  Conventional transformer-based large language models (LLMs) such as GPT and Claude operate through dense attention and probabilistic token prediction. These architectures generate fluent responses but lack symbolic scaffolding and explicit Vritti (mental state) modeling, recursive ontology flows, or folded truth resurfacing.
2.  As a result, these systems exhibit persistent limitations: (i) hallucination, where outputs deviate from factual content; (ii) inefficiency from reliance on full-context attention; (iii) inability to adjust delivery tone or emotional resonance; and (iv) limited explainability, since confidence appears only as logit spread rather than structured internal variables such as aspects (symbolic dimensional ontology), Guna (energy modes), and Kosha (awareness layers) entropies.
3.  Existing approaches such as retrieval augmentation, prompt engineering, and parameter scaling improve surface performance but remain confined to probabilistic methods. These approaches do not introduce structured ontologies, layered feedback, or symbolic purification, and thus cannot trace causes and consequences across reasoning layers or align outputs with readiness. They further lack governance gates such as domain entropy gating, cross-domain entropy gating, and Kosha readiness gating that ensure stability and symbolic fidelity.
4.  Accordingly, it is desired to have a framework that enables more stable, explainable reasoning, reduces hallucination, improves efficiency, and provides auditable internal states. Such a framework should incorporate syllable–Vritti decomposition, ontology mapping, entropy feedback across multiple dimensions, and runtime mechanisms such as mirror logic, experiential anchors, and a Deferred Insight Engine to resurface unresolved truths only under readiness conditions.
5.  By combining symbolic decomposition with entropy-driven control, the invention advances beyond conventional LLMs toward systems that are both technically grounded and capable of adaptive delivery through dual reasoning flows that balance executional clarity with recursive depth. This invention therefore introduces a symbolic reasoning architecture that integrates structured ontologies, entropy feedback, and governance mechanisms to deliver stable, transparent, and context-aligned outputs beyond the limits of transformer models.

Blockchain transactions associated with this application may be “endorsed” before being committed to the blockchain, while transactions that are not endorsed are disregarded.  
    An endorsement policy allows chaincodes to specify endorsers for a transaction in the form of a set of peer nodes that are necessary for endorsement. When a client sends the transaction to the peers specified in the endorsement policy, the transaction is executed to validate the transaction. After validation, the transactions enter an ordering phase in which a consensus protocol is applied to produce an ordered sequence of endorsed transactions grouped into blocks.
