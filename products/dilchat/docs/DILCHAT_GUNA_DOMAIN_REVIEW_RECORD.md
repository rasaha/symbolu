A reviewer may complete this record only if they hold, and can attest to, **all** of the following.

sub-tradition, and regionally divergent conventions — no single generalist competence is sufficient.

1. **Jyotisha (Vedic astrology) competence** — working command of rashi/nakshatra systems, the Lahiri
   sidereal framework, and graha (planetary) relationships.
2. **Muhurta / Melapaka (marriage-matching) competence** — direct familiarity with Ashtakoota Guna
   Milan as a practiced system, not only as summarized online.
3. **Sanskrit competence** — ability to read the normative Sanskrit text of *Muhurta Chintamani*
   (Melapaka Prakarana) directly, **or** a named Sanskrit-competent collaborator who does, so that
   the normative text governs over any English bridge on conflict.
4. **Traditional marriage-matching practice** — real experience adjudicating koota outcomes,
   doshas, and pariharas (cancellations), including how schools differ.
5. **Regional-variation competence** — ability to distinguish North-Indian Ashtakoota from
   South-Indian Dashakoota conventions and to keep them from being silently blended.

If any of the above cannot be attested, the reviewer must **decline** and leave this record
