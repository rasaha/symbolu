**No case on the hidden pilot required more than one answer-bearing operative that the
single-primary packet could not render.** Every competition resolved to a compatible
or resolved category (Table 10), and no genuine unresolved conflict arose. Therefore
this experiment does **not** demonstrate the packet cardinality contract as the active
bottleneck — that question is NOT YET ESTABLISHED on this corpus. Governance conflict,
operative-set multiplicity, and packet cardinality are reported separately and are not
collapsed: here all three are effectively inactive.
