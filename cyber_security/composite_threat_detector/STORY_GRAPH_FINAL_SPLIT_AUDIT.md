# Final-Split Independence Audit (Run 2) + Replacement Disposition

Evidence-based audit of whether the Run-2 "final" split
(`sha-256:b44671b8…`, commit `019e1f0d`) was genuinely untouched before freeze.
This record supersedes nothing; it adds a finding about Run 2's evaluation
integrity and defines the replacement split used by the verification run (Run 3).

## Question (§3)

Was the Run-2 final split inspected — directly or via aggregates — before the
configuration was frozen?

## Findings (code + command-history audit)

**The Run-2 final split was NOT independent. It was exposed.** Concretely, during
Run-2 development and *before* `freeze.build_freeze(... 'final')` was called:

1. `story_corpus_v2.evaluate_corpus()` was executed repeatedly over the **entire
   108-case corpus** (all splits) to validate labels and check separation. Those
   aggregate outputs — `benign_escalate_rate`, `benign_false_completion_rate`,
   category `Counter`s, the per-case table — **included final-split cases**.
2. The corpus code was **modified after** observing those full-corpus aggregates:
   the bank-assisted authorization's `covered_operations` was extended, and
   `B_preexistdev` was restructured, specifically to drive the benign-escalation
   aggregate to 0.0.
3. Several cases whose modeling changed after those observations land in the
   Run-2 final split: `B_family_0/1`, `B_medical_0/1/2`, `B_benefrecreate_0/1`,
   `B_bankbenef_1` (8 of 31 final cases).

Answering §3 precisely:

- **Which splits were evaluated before freeze?** All three (dev, calibration,
  **and final**) via full-corpus `evaluate_corpus()` calls.
- **Which metrics were inspected?** Full-corpus completion rates, benign ESCALATE
  rate, category distributions, and the per-case table — all spanning the final
  split.
- **Which changes were made afterward?** Legitimate-story coverage
  (`BANK_ASSISTED_TRANSFER_STORY` + the authorization scope) and the
  `B_preexistdev` case, plus the minimality-gate metric — after seeing full-corpus
  results.
- **Could those observations have influenced the final configuration?** **Yes.**
  The corpus and coverage model were tuned while final-split aggregates were
  visible. Independence cannot be claimed.

The Run-2 `run_final_split()` guard was real (it refuses on frozen-input drift),
but running the official runner once does **not** establish that the split was
unobserved beforehand — and it was observed.

## Disposition (§4) — Outcome B: final split exposed

1. The Run-2 final split is **marked exposed** and **retained as development
   evidence** (its hashes/metrics remain in the evidence ledger; nothing is
   deleted).
2. A **replacement final split (Run 3)** is generated by a **frozen generator
   version** (`ctd.storycorpus.gen/2.0.0`) under a **new deterministic seed**
   (`HOLDOUT_SEED`), with **no manual scenario selection** after generation
   (`story_corpus_v2.holdout_final_cases()`).
3. **No code path evaluates the replacement final split before the official frozen
   run.** `evaluate_corpus()` / `check_gates()` / the dev+calibration commands
   operate on the development corpus only and cannot reach the holdout by
   construction; only the guarded `final_eval.run_official_final()` evaluates it,
   after `freeze.require_frozen(official=True)`.
4. The holdout **case-id hash, generator version, and seed are frozen** before
   execution; the guarded runner writes **one immutable evaluation record**.
5. The replacement final split is run **exactly once**, after freeze, and whatever
   it reports is recorded verbatim.

This audit is itself development-time reasoning: it was written before the Run-3
holdout was ever evaluated, and the holdout's *outcomes* were not inspected to
produce it (only its construction, which follows validated Run-2 family logic with
new entity indices).
