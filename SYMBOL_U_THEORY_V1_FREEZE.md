# SYMBOL_U_THEORY_V1_FREEZE

> **Purpose:** freeze the assumptions so engineering can proceed. **No new theory, no new
> ontological layers.** This freeze marks the transition from theory-expansion mode to
> architecture-design mode. It is **not** a validation — current evidence is largely
> *against* the meaning claim (O1.5 failed; corpus preliminary near-null). We freeze
> because further theory expansion has diminishing returns and the only way forward is to
> test, cheaply. Companion: `SYMBOL_U_IMPLEMENTABLE_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md`.

## 1. Frozen assumptions (v1)

- **Finite unit inventory** `Σ` (varṇa/phoneme), treated as **alphabet-agnostic**.
- **Per-unit operators + ordered composition:** `ρ(σ₁…σₙ) = M_{σₙ}∘⋯∘M_{σ₁}(s₀)` — the
  monoid action; the **linear/matrix** form is the identifiable special case used in v1.
- **Order-sensitivity (non-commutativity)** is the distinctive structural commitment.
- **Latent reading state with linear observable readouts.**
- v1 operates in **one modality** (speech/text). Multi-view is the validation *target*,
  not a v1 build.

## 2. Explicitly out of scope (v1)

Cross-modal / neural / motor measurement; LLM control & policy translation; any
"states of reality" / physics / consciousness claims; *assumed* Sanskrit privilege; a
"Level-0 generating principle" beyond the factorization itself; CSR / global-resonance as
a fixpoint. All deferred or rejected.

## 3. Treated as hypotheses, not truths

- A finite latent inventory underlies meaning. *(untested; O1.5 near-null)*
- Order-sensitivity carries usable signal. *(untested at unit level)*
- The inventory factors into `k ≪ n` primitives. *(hypothesis)*
- The structure is cross-modally invariant. *(the original claim; untested)*
- Sanskrit varṇa is a privileged chart. *(comparative; unsupported so far)*

## 4. Required for implementation (the minimal kernel)

Finite inventory · per-unit operators · ordered composition · a latent reading state ·
linear readouts. Nothing else.

## 5. Future research (not v1)

Human pseudoword **order-effect** data; cross-modal factor alignment; symmetry/gesture
*derivation* of operators (only if it predicts); CSR enrichment; varṇa-vs-IPA-vs-data
factor comparison.

## 6. What would falsify or weaken v1

- Composition produces **no structure beyond a bag** (order-effects ≈ 0) → non-commutativity
  claim dead.
- Readings **track sound, not meaning** (O1.5 re-fail) → the *input decomposition* is the
  bottleneck, not the engine.
- **No stable low-rank factorization** → factorization refinement dead.
- **Random / relabel operators perform equally** → the specific inventory carries no
  information.

**Honest status at freeze.** v1 is frozen on *weak empirical footing*. The reading already
failed once (O1.5: sound-driven, beaten by a sentiment word-list), and the forensic cause
was **upstream** (grapheme→varṇa decomposition + bag aggregation). v1 fixes the aggregation
(operator product, not bag) but **inherits the same input decomposition**, so the meaning
claim remains at risk by construction. The freeze is a decision to *stop expanding theory
and start testing structure cheaply* — not a claim that the theory is supported.
