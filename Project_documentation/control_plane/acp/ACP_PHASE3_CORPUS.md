## 3. Determinism & honesty

- LIVE scenarios call the real `TaskPlanner` at run time (deterministic).
- RECORDED scenarios seed `np.random` before each MPC call (deterministic).
- The `RECORDED` ground truth is the real validator's own verdict (no independent
  oracle); those scenarios are excluded from authored recall/false-rejection and
  scored by ACP-vs-validator agreement instead.
- No threshold is tuned on the corpus; all physical thresholds are the real
  validator defaults.
