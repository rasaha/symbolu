# Consent Summary

You are invited to take part in a behavioral instrumentation pilot. Please read this summary. Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time.

What we record: the TIMING of your keyboard and mouse activity while you use this study page, the CATEGORY of keys you press (for example: letter, digit, space, backspace) — never the actual characters or any text you type — normalized mouse positions and clicks, and the current task step.

What we do NOT record: the words, sentences, passwords, or any text you type; anything you do outside this page; your screen contents, files, or browsing. No system-wide keyboard or mouse monitoring is installed — collection happens only inside this page and only while a task is running.

How data is handled: data is stored locally and pseudonymously (a random code, not your name). You can delete your session immediately after it finishes, using the delete button on the completion screen. You may decline now, or stop during any task.

Purpose and limits: this phase measures whether we can COLLECT good-quality, synchronized, privacy-safe timing data. It does NOT compute or show any identity or biometric result. Behavioral timing data can still be personal information; we do not claim it is anonymous.

By confirming consent you agree to this recording for this research pilot. If you do not consent, close this page — no data will be collected.
